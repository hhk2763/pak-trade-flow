# Go-to-market: first 5 paying customers

_Drafted 2026-10-18. Everything below is a hypothesis to test with real
conversations, not a conclusion. Update it as answers come in._

## 1. The bet in one paragraph

PSX equity analysts need **early, specific evidence** of what listed companies
are importing and exporting: cement makers' coal and clinker, refiners' crude
and furnace oil, fuel marketers' petrol and diesel, fertilizer inputs, PIBT's
own throughput. Today they get it from monthly PBS/APCMA/OCAC releases weeks
after the fact, or not at all. Port Qasim and KPT publish the raw data **daily**,
in PDFs nobody has time to read. We read every one, count each vessel once,
and map it to tickers. **We sell a weekly 3-page report; the dashboard is the
proof the data is real.**

## 2. Who we sell to first, and why

| | PSX research (brokerages, AMCs) | Commodity importers / traders | Shipping & logistics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | Need earnings/volume signals before results and monthly releases | Need to see competitors' incoming supply before setting prices | Congestion, berth planning |
| What we have for it | Signals page, ticker mapping, PIBT throughput | Anchorage list: who is bringing what, how much, from where | Anchorage wait by terminal |
| Reachability | High: concentrated in Karachi, on LinkedIn, used to buying research | Medium: relationship-driven, WhatsApp | Medium |
| Willingness to pay | Medium-high, small budgets but recurring | High, if it moves a trade | Low-medium |
| **Order** | **1st** | 2nd (same data, different framing) | Later |

The reason for going first with research is not that they pay the most. It is
that they are the **fastest to reach and to say yes or no**, which is what we
need in the next 6 weeks.

## 3. What we actually know the data can and cannot support

Say these out loud in every conversation. A buyer who finds a limitation
we did not mention stops trusting everything else.

- **Can:** vessel-level imports and exports at both ports, daily, from
  2026-03-07 (KPT) and 2026-04-28 (PQ); manifest tonnage counted once per
  vessel; who is waiting at PQ anchorage right now, with cargo, origin, agent
  and days waiting; how long vessels wait by terminal.
- **Cannot (yet):** history before March 2026 (no year-on-year, no
  seasonality claims); KPT anchorage (KPT does not publish one); which company
  owns a cargo (the source names the shipping agent, not the consignee);
  anything outside PQ and KPT (Gwadar, land routes).
- **Watch-outs:** sources sometimes fail for days (KPT's site has been broken
  since 2026-09-30; the PDFs still arrive). LNG is printed in m³ and converted.
  Small samples: one vessel can swing a commodity's 28-day total, so the
  report hides percentages under 3 vessels.

## 4. Offer and pricing (to test, not to announce)

- **Founding customer offer:** 4 weekly reports free, then **PKR 35,000/month
  per firm** (about USD 125) for the weekly PDF + dashboard access for up to 3
  people, **locked for 12 months** for the first 5 firms. Invoice monthly, bank
  transfer. No contracts longer than a month until the product earns it.
- **Anchors to test in conversation:** "Would PKR 25k / 50k / 100k a month be
  cheap, fair or a non-starter for your desk?" Ask about the budget they
  already spend on data (Bloomberg seats, local data vendors, paid
  newsletters) rather than what they would like to pay.
- **Upsell to test later:** per-company deep dives (e.g. a monthly PIBTL or
  refinery volume note), Excel/CSV export, a daily WhatsApp alert when a
  tracked flow moves.

Price per firm, not per seat. Research desks share everything internally
anyway, and per-firm pricing makes the yes easier.

## 6. Outreach scripts

**LinkedIn / email (cold), keep it under 90 words:**

> Hi [name], I track Pakistan's port cargo vessel by vessel from Port Qasim
> and KPT's daily statements and map it to PSX tickers. Last 28 days, for
> example: clinker exports +30% (17 vessels), crude imports +28%, furnace oil
> exports −38%, and 172k t of palm oil queued at PQ anchorage with waits up to
> 14 days at LCT. I'm giving a few research desks a free weekly report for 4
> weeks in exchange for blunt feedback. Can I send you this week's?
> Sample attached. — [you]

**WhatsApp (warm intro):**

> Assalam o alaikum [name], [mutual] said you cover [cement / OMCs /
> fertilizer]. I've built a weekly port-cargo tracker for PSX names, counting
> every vessel at PQ and KPT. 3-page PDF attached (this week's). Would 15
> minutes this week work to hear what would make it useful for your coverage?

**Follow-up (day 4, once):**

> Quick one: did anything in the report surprise you, or was it all already
> known? Either answer helps me.

Always attach the latest real report, never a mock-up. Update the numbers in
the message to that week's takeaways.

## 7. Discovery interview (15–20 minutes)

The goal is to learn about their **current behaviour**, not to pitch. Ask
about the past, not hypotheticals. Write the answers down the same day.

1. Walk me through how you built your last volume estimate for [a company you
   cover]. What did you use? Where did it come from? How late was it?
2. When did port or shipping data last change a call you made, or would have?
3. What do you pay for today that's closest to this? Who approved it?
4. *(Show the report.)* Which line here would you actually use? Which would
   you ignore? What's missing?
5. Is weekly the right rhythm, or would you want an alert the day something
   moves?
6. If this landed every Monday, who else on your desk would read it?
7. *(Last, once.)* We're taking 5 founding firms at PKR 35k/month after a free
   month. Is that a conversation worth having with whoever signs off?

**Good signs:** they name a specific stock and decision; they forward it
internally without being asked; they ask for history or a CSV; they ask
"how much".
**Bad signs:** "interesting", "send it over", no follow-up questions, and
"we'd need years of history" (that one is a real blocker; log it).

## 8. Milestones and kill criteria

| By | Target | If missed |
|---|---|---|
| Week 2 | 15 conversations held; written notes for each | Outreach problem, not product. Fix the message, ask for intros |
| Week 4 | 6+ firms receiving the free report and opening it | Value problem. Re-read notes; adjust the signals |
| Week 6 | **2 firms committed to pay** (invoice issued) | Test the importer segment with the anchorage list before building anything new |
| Week 10 | 5 paying firms, under 1 day a week to produce | Then invest: history backfill, alerts, exports |

Stop building features until week 6 unless a conversation demands one. The
product is ahead of the evidence that anyone will pay for it.

## 9. Weekly operating routine (~1 hour, Monday)

1. Check the nightly ETL went green and the site shows the latest date.
2. Open `/report?key=…`, read every takeaway against the tables. Fix or drop
   anything that looks wrong (a misparse is worse than a missing line).
3. Add one or two sentences of your own context at the top if something is
   newsworthy (the report itself never guesses at causes).
4. Print to PDF, send to the list, log who opened and replied.
